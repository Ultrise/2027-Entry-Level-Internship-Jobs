## Days Left

The countdown is based on each job's expiration date.

- `25d` means 25 full days remaining.
- `3d` means 3 full days remaining.
- `0d` means less than 24 hours remaining.
- `N/A` means no expiration date is available.
- Expired jobs are automatically excluded.

Current listings do not include expiration dates from the source, so they display `N/A`.
